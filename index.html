About the song

Day by Day is a diary set to a beat — the unglamorous math of hustle in a city that never quite lets you rest.

Beneath the skyline views and neon nights sits a quieter question: who's actually there for you when the day is done? The track traces the loop of grinding, doubting, and starting again — climbing stairs with no top floor, running in place on a treadmill of routine — while holding onto the people who've stuck around through it.

It's a letter to whoever's still figuring out their own version of "making it," co-produced with Gummy B, built one track at a time, one day at a time.
